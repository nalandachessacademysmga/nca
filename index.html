About Us

Nalanda Chess Academy is dedicated to fostering a love for chess and nurturing talent from beginners to advanced players. We provide a supportive environment for players to develop their skills, engage in friendly competition, and connect with a passionate community.

Our commitment is to make chess accessible and enjoyable for everyone, promoting strategic thinking and personal growth through this timeless game.


Srikrishna Udupa, President and Head Coach for Nalanda Chess Academy has been instrumental in training new generation of chess champions. He is a renowned chess coach and a great motivational speaker who has dedicated 30+ years of his life in training 100s of young chess talents all over the world.


His story telling ability coupled with his chess knowledge has always attracted chess students. He has conducted numerous chess coaching camps all over India and even trained students from abroad in the online coaching classes. His students have been extremely successful and earned laurels all over the world.
